# Source ledger

Research date: 1 September 2026

## Quality tiers

- **A — primary/official:** legislation, regulator guidance, official statistics and official technical policy.
- **B — first-party product:** competitor feature, pricing, policy or current inventory page. Authoritative for what the company displays, not for independent performance claims.
- **C — directional experience:** vendor survey, app-store review or user discussion. Useful for hypothesis formation, not population truth.

## E. Search distribution and technical policy

63. **A — Google JobPosting structured-data policy.** Requires real open jobs, usable application paths and timely expiry; disallows unauthorized postings on behalf of organizations. [Source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting)
64. **A — Google Indexing API.** Job sites can notify Google when job pages are added, updated or removed; intended for short-lived pages. [Source](https://developers.google.com/search/apis/indexing-api/v3/quickstart)
65. **A — Google Search spam policies.** Scaled scraped or generated pages without added value can be treated as abuse. [Source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

## Source-use rules for the project

1. Re-check all price, inventory and regulatory-status claims at the moment they influence a launch decision.
2. Do not convert a competitor claim into an independently verified performance fact.
3. Do not convert an app review into a population statistic.
4. Do not treat public access, structured data or robots allowance as publication permission.
5. Obtain qualified German/EU advice before finalizing source ingestion, outreach, placement, platform-work or employee-leasing boundaries.
